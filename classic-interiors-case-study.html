Overview

What this project is

Classic Interior Design Studio designs homes and commercial spaces around Attur and Salem. Clients choose a designer by looking at past work, so the studio needed a site that showed its projects clearly and made it simple to get in touch.

The Challenge

Selling design without being able to show it in person

THE PROBLEM

Interior design is judged visually. The studio had strong project photos but no central place to show them, and prospects relied on scattered social posts and word of mouth.

THE SOLUTION

A hand-coded HTML/CSS/JS multi-page site where photo galleries lead, supported by clear service descriptions and a simple contact form, with no CMS overhead so pages load quickly.

Challenges & Learnings

What came up, and how I handled it

01

Large project photos vs. page speed

Interior photography is high resolution, and a gallery of full-size images slows a page down, especially on mobile data.

→ [EDIT: e.g. resized and compressed images, lazy-loaded the gallery]
02

Keeping galleries consistent across pages

Photos came in mixed sizes and orientations, which made grids look uneven.

→ [EDIT: e.g. fixed aspect-ratio grid with object-fit cropping]
03

Making contact effortless

Local clients often prefer WhatsApp or a quick call over filling a long form.

→ [EDIT: e.g. short form plus WhatsApp and Instagram links in the footer]
