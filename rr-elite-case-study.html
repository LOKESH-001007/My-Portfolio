Overview

What this project is

RR Elite Auto Care is a multi-brand garage in Sanniyasigundu, Salem that handles engine work, AC, electrical, denting, painting, wheel care and detailing for every make and model. They needed a website that said "we take any car, any job" clearly — and made it effortless to call, WhatsApp or find the workshop.

The Challenge

Earning trust when a car breaks down

THE PROBLEM

Someone searching for a garage is usually stressed and on their phone. Most local garage sites bury the phone number, list services in a vague wall of text, and give no sign they will actually work on your brand — so the visitor leaves and calls the next result.

THE SOLUTION

A hand-coded four-page HTML/CSS/JS site with the call and WhatsApp buttons always one tap away, a scrolling strip of supported brands, services grouped into clear categories, and a straight-talking "why us" section that answers the real objection: will you take my car?

Challenges & Learnings

What came up, and how I handled it

01

Making "all brands, all jobs" believable

Every garage claims to fix everything. A plain sentence on the page wouldn't convince a visitor whose car is on the line.

→ Showed proof instead of claiming it: a moving brand strip, categorised services, and an explicit "you approve the cost before we work" promise.
02

Designing for people who want to call, not read

Most visitors arrive on mobile with a problem and little patience for scrolling through paragraphs.

→ Put two tap-to-call numbers and a WhatsApp button in the header, hero, footer and a floating button, so contact is never more than a tap away.
03

Getting found locally without a CMS

The client's real competition is other Salem garages on Google, and a static site has no SEO plugin to lean on.

→ Wrote page-specific titles and descriptions, Open Graph and Twitter tags, and a canonical URL by hand, with the workshop address spelled out on-page.
